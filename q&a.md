# 1. General & Executive (Non-Technical)

### Q1.1: What is ORCA in simple terms?
**Answer:**
ORCA is an AI-powered maritime intelligence workstation. It collects live ocean data (wave heights, ocean currents, wind, water temperature, chlorophyll levels) from satellite constellations and weather supercomputers, analyzes whether conditions are safe or dangerous, and gives clear advice to fishermen, port authorities, and researchers—even speaking out loud in regional languages like Hindi and Marathi.

### Q1.2: What real-world problem does ORCA solve?
**Answer:**
Historically, marine weather data has been fragmented, hard to read, and presented in complex scientific formats. Artisanal fishermen often venture into dangerous waters without knowing that high swells or storms are approaching, leading to accidents and loss of life. At the same time, commercial vessels burn excessive fuel navigating suboptimal routes. ORCA simplifies complex satellite data into an intuitive traffic-light safety score (0–100) and actionable voice alerts that anyone can understand.

### Q1.3: Who are the target users of ORCA?
**Answer:**
1. **Artisanal & Coastal Fishermen:** Need simple, hands-free voice guidance in their native language about whether to launch small motorized boats (OBMs) and where safe, fertile fishing zones exist.
2. **Commercial Fleets & Trawlers:** Need 7-day sea state predictions, tidal heights, and fuel-efficient, safe routing corridors.
3. **Maritime Authorities & Coast Guards:** Need real-time surveillance, vessel safety ratings, incident audits, and regulatory zone enforcement.
4. **Marine Biologists & Oceanographers:** Need raw bio-optical telemetry, chlorophyll trophic fronts, and sea surface temperature anomalies to monitor marine ecosystems.

### Q1.4: How is ORCA different from standard weather apps like AccuWeather or Google Weather?
**Answer:**
Standard weather apps focus almost exclusively on terrestrial atmospheric conditions (air temperature, rain in cities). They do not provide:
- **Ocean physics:** Significant wave height, peak wave period, swell direction, surface current velocity, or tidal flood/ebb heights.
- **Ocean biology:** Satellite chlorophyll-a biomass indicating phytoplankton blooms.
- **Vessel-specific safety:** Differentiated safety status for small fiberglass boats vs. large steel-hull commercial trawlers.
- **Scientific data integrity:** Explicit data trust consensus ratings and provenance tracking.

# 2. Marine Domain & Oceanographic Science

### Q2.1: What is "Significant Wave Height" ($H_s$) and why is it critical?
**Answer:**
Significant wave height ($H_s$ or $H_{1/3}$) is defined as the mean wave height of the highest one-third of all waves recorded during an observation window (typically 20–30 minutes). It closely matches what an experienced mariner observes visually. Waves higher than $H_s$ (often up to $1.8\times$ or $2\times$ during rogue wave events) frequently occur, making $H_s$ the primary factor in determining whether small craft will capsize.

### Q2.2: What is the difference between Wind Waves and Swell Waves?
**Answer:**
- **Wind Waves (Chop):** Generated locally by the immediate wind blowing across the local sea surface. They are typically chaotic, short-period (3–6 seconds), and steep.
- **Swell Waves:** Waves generated by distant storm systems hundreds or thousands of miles away that have traveled across deep ocean basins. They are organized, smooth, long-crested, and long-period (8–20 seconds). Long-period swells carry massive energy and create dangerous breaking surf near shallow coastal shoals.

### Q2.3: Why is Chlorophyll-a Biomass important in marine intelligence?
**Answer:**
Chlorophyll-a is the primary photosynthetic pigment in microscopic phytoplankton. Phytoplankton is the foundation of the marine food web. Where ocean currents converge or upwelling brings cold, nutrient-rich water to the surface, chlorophyll concentrations spike ($>1.5\text{ mg/m}^3$). This attracts zooplankton, which attracts pelagic fish schools (mackerel, tuna, sardines). Identifying chlorophyll fronts enables fishermen to pinpoint Potential Fishing Zones (PFZs), cutting fuel burn and search times.

### Q2.4: How does the coastal tide model work in ORCA?
**Answer:**
ORCA implements a semi-diurnal astronomical tidal calculation based on the primary lunar constituent ($M_2$), which has an average period of **12 hours and 25.2 minutes (12.42 hours)**:
$$\theta(t) = \frac{2\pi \cdot (t - t_0)}{12.42 \cdot 3600}$$
$$\text{Tide Height } h(t) = h_{\text{mean}} + A \cdot \sin(\theta(t))$$
From the rate of change $\frac{dh}{dt} = A \cdot \omega \cos(\theta(t))$, ORCA determines:
- **Flood Tide (Rising):** $\frac{dh}{dt} > 0$
- **Ebb Tide (Falling):** $\frac{dh}{dt} < 0$
- Next High Tide & Low Tide timestamps and peak water depths.

# 3. System Architecture & Data Pipelines

### Q3.1: Can you describe the end-to-end data flow when a user clicks on the map?
**Answer:**
1. **User Interaction:** The user clicks coordinates $(lat, lon)$ on the Leaflet map.
2. **Client Validation:** The client updates the active coordinate state and dispatches a `POST /api/analysis` request with `{ latitude, longitude }`.
3. **Geospatial Land-Sea Guard:** The server's `GeospatialService` checks whether the point falls on land or sea using global elevation models and boundary bounds.
4. **Parallel Telemetry Fetch:** If marine, the server triggers concurrent asynchronous fetches:
   - `MarineProvider`: Queries Copernicus/ECMWF 0.25° grid via Open-Meteo for waves, swell, period, and ocean currents.
   - `WeatherProvider`: Queries DWD/NOAA for 10m wind, gusts, pressure, temperature, and 7-day extended forecasts.
   - `ChlorophyllProvider`: Queries Sentinel-3 OLCI ocean color telemetry.
5. **Deterministic Processing:** The raw data feeds into the Risk Engine, Maritime Suitability Engine, Tide Modeling Engine, and Evidence Graph generator.
6. **Audit & Cache:** The resulting `FullAnalysisBundle` is cached and logged in the immutable audit store.
7. **Client Rendering:** The client receives JSON, updating the map layers, the 3-tab intelligence side panel, and feeding the AI assistant's context.

### Q3.2: What happens if an external data provider is slow or down?
**Answer:**
ORCA utilizes a resilient multi-tier fallback:
1. **Request Timeouts:** Provider HTTP requests are configured with strict timeouts (5–8s) to prevent thread exhaustion.
2. **Cached Snapshots:** If a live provider fails, the system serves the latest cached snapshot for that sector and marks the observation with a `CACHED` status badge.
3. **Data Trust Passport Degradation:** The Data Trust score automatically docks confidence points (e.g., dropping from 94% Live Consensus to 65% Cached), transparently alerting operators that readings are unverified.

### Q3.3: Why does ORCA use Vite instead of Create React App or Next.js?
**Answer:**
- **Lightning-Fast HMR & Bundling:** Vite leverages native ES modules (ESM) during development, eliminating multi-minute re-bundling cycles.
- **Lightweight Static Build:** Generates an ultra-optimized client distribution (`dist/`) that can be served directly by Express or distributed over edge CDNs with minimal server overhead.
- **Strict Control Over Architecture:** Avoids unnecessary server-side rendering (SSR) complexity for a real-time SPA that relies on client-side WebGL Leaflet mapping and Web Speech audio APIs.

# 4. Mathematical Inference & Scoring Algorithms

### Q4.1: How is the Modeled Maritime Risk Score (0–100) calculated?
**Answer:**
The Risk Score is computed deterministically by normalizing and weighting physical oceanographic hazard variables against standard maritime safety thresholds:
$$\text{Risk} = \min\left(100, \sum w_i \cdot f_i(x_i)\right)$$
Where:
1. **Wave Hazard Weight ($35\%$):** $\frac{\text{Wave Height (m)}}{3.0\text{ m}} \times 100$
2. **Wind Velocity Weight ($30\%$):** $\frac{\text{Wind Speed (km/h)}}{50.0\text{ km/h}} \times 100$
3. **Ocean Current Shear Weight ($20\%$):** $\frac{\text{Current (km/h)}}{3.5\text{ km/h}} \times 100$
4. **Precipitation & Squall Weight ($15\%$):** $\frac{\text{Precipitation (mm)}}{20.0\text{ mm}} \times 100$

- **Score 0–25:** **LOW** (Safe conditions)
- **Score 26–50:** **MODERATE** (Caution advised for small craft)
- **Score 51–75:** **ELEVATED** (Hazardous; small craft restricted)
- **Score 76–100:** **HIGH** (Severe storm/gale; all vessels seek shelter)

### Q4.2: How is the Maritime Suitability Score (0–100) calculated?
**Answer:**
Suitability represents the operational favorability for navigation and fishing:
$$\text{Suitability} = 100 - (\text{Risk} \times 0.6) - (\text{Trophic Absence Penalty}) - (\text{Thermal Penalty})$$
- If Chlorophyll-a is rich ($> 2.0\text{ mg/m}^3$) and Sea Surface Temperature is in the optimal range ($27^\circ\text{C} - 29.5^\circ\text{C}$), a biological favorability bonus is applied.
- If waves are rough ($> 2.0\text{ m}$) or wind exceeds $35\text{ km/h}$, suitability drops severely.

### Q4.3: How does the Vessel Departure & Craft Workability algorithm classify vessels?
**Answer:**
It evaluates each craft class against nautical tolerance thresholds:
1. **Small Craft / OBM (< 25 ft):**
   - `SAFE`: Wave $< 1.2\text{ m}$, Wind $< 20\text{ km/h}$, Gusts $< 30\text{ km/h}$.
   - `CAUTION`: Wave $1.2\text{ m} - 1.8\text{ m}$ or Wind $20 - 32\text{ km/h}$.
   - `HAZARDOUS`: Wave $> 1.8\text{ m}$ or Wind $> 32\text{ km/h}$ or Squall/Thunderstorm.
2. **Commercial Trawler / Deep-Sea:**
   - `SAFE`: Wave $< 2.8\text{ m}$, Wind $< 45\text{ km/h}$.
   - `CAUTION`: Wave $2.8\text{ m} - 3.8\text{ m}$.
   - `HAZARDOUS`: Wave $> 3.8\text{ m}$ or Tropical Cyclone warnings.
3. **Sailing Craft:**
   - Evaluates minimum and maximum sailing wind envelopes ($12\text{ km/h} \le \text{Wind} \le 35\text{ km/h}$).

# 5. Geospatial Engine & Terrestrial Land-Masking

### Q5.1: What is "Wise Terrestrial Land-Masking" and why was it necessary?
**Answer:**
Standard oceanographic satellite products (such as Sentinel-3 OLCI or MODIS Ocean Color) produce inaccurate or invalid data when sampled over land (vegetation reflectance mimics chlorophyll). Naive apps display high chlorophyll numbers over cities and mountains.
ORCA implements **Wise Land-Masking**:
- The server checks elevation contours and known land bounds.
- If elevation $> 5\text{ meters}$ inland, the point is classified as `TERRESTRIAL_LAND`.
- Chlorophyll is strictly set to `0.00 mg/m³` with `status: LAND_MASKED`.
- The frontend suppresses ocean biological dispersion heatmaps and displays an informative `LAND MASKED` badge.

### Q5.2: How does ORCA achieve zero watermarks on its maps?
**Answer:**
Instead of using trial tiers of commercial tile providers (like CartoDB or Mapbox) that inject "API KEY REQUIRED" or watermarks across the map canvas:
- ORCA uses **ArcGIS World Ocean Basemap** and **Esri World Imagery** public REST tile services.
- These tiles provide clean, unwatermarked high-resolution satellite and bathymetric layers with zero API keys required, ensuring 100% uptime for public safety tools.

# 6. Artificial Intelligence, Gemini & Multilingual Voice

### Q6.1: Which AI model does ORCA use, and how is it integrated?
**Answer:**
ORCA is powered by Google's state-of-the-art **`gemini-3.6-flash`** model through the official `@google/genai` SDK. The model is primed with a specialized system instruction that establishes it as a maritime environmental intelligence expert. When a query is made, the backend automatically injects the active location's real-time buoy observations (waves, currents, wind, risk, suitability, candidate zones) into the prompt context, preventing hallucinations.

### Q6.2: How did you fix the problem of raw asterisks in chat responses?
**Answer:**
LLMs routinely output markdown formatting like `**bold text**` and `*bullet points*`.
In ORCA, two layers of sanitation were engineered:
1. **Visual Markdown Parser (`markdownHelper.tsx`):**
   A regex scanner transforms paired asterisks (`**text**`) into semantic `<strong>` elements and cleans single asterisks (`*item`) into proper bullet structures. Unpaired stray asterisks are completely removed.
2. **Text-To-Speech Sanitizer (`cleanTextForSpeech`):**
   Before passing text to the browser's speech synthesis engine, all markdown characters (`*`, `#`, `_`, `[`, `]`, `\``) are stripped out so the synthesizer never pronounces "asterisk asterisk" aloud.

### Q6.3: How does the Multilingual Regional Voice Assistant work?
**Answer:**
- **Speech Recognition:** Uses the HTML5 Web Speech Recognition API configured with Indian regional BCP-47 locale codes:
  - English: `en-IN`
  - Marathi: `mr-IN`
  - Hindi: `hi-IN`
  - Gujarati: `gu-IN`, Tamil: `ta-IN`, Telugu: `te-IN`, etc.
- **Audio Visualizer:** An HTML5 Canvas renders a glowing gradient orb with real-time sinusoids and dynamic particles that pulse with voice volume.
- **Speech Synthesis:** Synthesizes Gemini's localized safety response using matched regional voices (`mr-IN` or `hi-IN`).

# 7. Frontend Engineering, GIS Cartography & UX Design

### Q7.1: How did you eliminate the "Upward UI Shift" bug in the chatbot?
**Answer:**
- **The Cause:** The previous code called `messagesEndRef.current?.scrollIntoView({ behavior: 'smooth' })`. In modern browsers, `scrollIntoView()` bubbles up through all ancestor containers all the way to `window`, shifting the entire page upward and trapping the top navigation bar offscreen.
- **The Solution:**
  1. Replaced `scrollIntoView()` with container-level scrolling:
     `scrollContainerRef.current.scrollTo({ top: scrollHeight, behavior: 'smooth' })`.
  2. Pinned the root viewport with `.app-shell { position: fixed; top: 0; left: 0; right: 0; bottom: 0; overflow: hidden; }`.
  3. Added an explicit viewport boundary reset: `if (window.scrollY !== 0) window.scrollTo(0, 0)`.

### Q7.2: How was the side panel simplified to avoid overlapping badges?
**Answer:**
- In the original design, `MODELED RISK` and `DETERMINISTIC` were placed in a horizontal flexbox container inside a 140px card, forcing the text to wrap and overlap.
- We re-engineered the component into a vertical card layout:
  - Header with title and tiny uppercase type badge.
  - Large, bold numerical readout (e.g., `17` or `90`) with dedicated HSL color coding.
  - Status pill (e.g., `LOW RISK`, `EXCELLENT`) and primary driver description on dedicated lines.
- Segmented the entire side panel into 3 clear tabs (**`🌊 Marine`**, **`🌦️ Weather & Predictions`**, **`🧬 Bio`**) to eliminate endless vertical scrolling.

### Q7.3: How is map zooming made smooth and controllable?
**Answer:**
Leaflet's defaults zoom in $2\times$ jumps on slight trackpad movements. We fine-tuned the Leaflet map configuration:
- `wheelPxPerZoomLevel: 160` (slows mouse wheel sensitivity by $2.7\times$).
- `zoomDelta: 0.5` (allows fractional $0.5$ zoom steps instead of abrupt $1.0$ jumps).
- `zoomSnap: 0.5` (enables fractional zoom snapping).
- `wheelDebounceTime: 60` (buffers rapid trackpad momentum).

# 8. Authentication, RBAC & Security

### Q8.1: How does Firebase Authentication work in ORCA?
**Answer:**
ORCA implements Firebase Auth supporting:
- **Google OAuth Popup:** One-click enterprise authentication.
- **Email / Password:** Traditional registration and login.
- **Guest / Anonymous Mode:** Instant access for exploratory users without friction.

### Q8.2: How does Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) protect sensitive features?
**Answer:**
The system defines 4 distinct operational roles:
1. `GUEST`: Can explore maps, search locations, inspect metrics. Gated from running Scenario perturbations or exporting official decision packages.
2. `FISHERMAN`: Optimized for high-contrast alerts, regional voice guidance, and PFZ corridors.
3. `RESEARCHER`: Full access to raw bio-optical satellite telemetry, spectral curves, and time-series history.
4. `MARITIME_AUTHORITY`: Administrative controls, vessel departure clearance, port safety broadcasting, and audit log inspection.

When a Guest attempts an authorized action, a clean modal prompts them to authenticate.

# 9. Viva, Hackathon Defense & Tough Technical Questions

### Q9.1: "How can you prove your data isn't fake or hardcoded mock JSON?"
**Answer:**
"Our server communicates directly with the Open-Meteo and Copernicus APIs in real time. We can prove this live:
1. Inspect the Network Tab in DevTools: look at the `POST /api/analysis` payload.
2. Click any location across the globe (e.g., North Sea vs. Bay of Bengal vs. Arabian Sea). The wave height, wind speeds, and sea temperatures immediately reflect live meteorological conditions at those exact coordinates.
3. Check the server console logs: you will see real HTTP requests dispatched to `marine-api.open-meteo.com` and `api.open-meteo.com` returning timestamps within the last 15 minutes."

### Q9.2: "What happens if a user searches for an inland city like New Delhi or Kansas?"
**Answer:**
"ORCA's `GeospatialService` checks the coordinates against global elevation and boundary models. It returns an HTTP 422 with `code: LAND_LOCATION_REJECTED` and a clear message explaining that maritime analysis is restricted to ocean and coastal waters. If a near-coastal inland point is analyzed, our Wise Land-Masking automatically suppresses ocean bio-optics and sets Chlorophyll to `0.00 mg/m³`."

### Q9.3: "Is ORCA liable if a vessel sails and encounters a rogue wave?"
**Answer:**
"No. ORCA is designed as an advisory decision-support system. Every screen, snapshot export, and API payload carries an explicit legal and navigational disclaimer stating that modeled scores are decision aids and do not replace official government Notices to Mariners (NOTAMs), coast guard directives, or licensed navigational equipment."

### Q9.4: "How does the system scale if thousands of fishermen query the API simultaneously?"
**Answer:**
1. **Spatial In-Memory Caching:** Marine weather grids operate on a $0.25^\circ \times 0.25^\circ$ resolution ($\approx 25\text{ km}$). Coordinates falling within the same grid cell share the same cached response for 15 minutes, reducing upstream API calls by $>90\%$.
2. **Stateless API Architecture:** The Express server is stateless, allowing horizontal scaling behind standard load balancers (e.g., NGINX, AWS ALB).
3. **Static Client Distribution:** The frontend is a static bundle delivered through edge CDNs with zero client-rendering compute on the backend.
